## Product messaging cues

When reviewing body copy, check that Weglot's core product positioning is reinforced
where it's relevant — especially in sections describing manual steps that Weglot
automates. Flag as NEEDS REVIEW if a passage implies significant manual effort
without acknowledging Weglot removes that burden.

Key messages to look for:

- **Automation:** "detects and translates automatically", "no dev support needed",
  "set-and-forget"
- **Speed:** WordPress under 5 mins; all other CMS under 10 mins.
  Source: Top Reminders (27/01/26). Do not allow longer timelines to be cited.
- **Continuous translation:** new content auto-translated on publish; multilingual
  versions never go stale
- **AI Translation Model:** trains on brand voice, glossary, and manual edits;
  produces brand-aligned output from the first translation — no pre-training needed

## Table cell formatting

In Weglot articles, **Explanation/Description column cells** in two-column tables
should start with a **lowercase letter**. They read as continuation phrases from
the label in the adjacent cell, not as standalone sentences.

Confirmed in WEGBLOG013 editor review, May 2026. Cross-check against the
client-agnostic §3 rule before flagging.
